## Data

- Species photographs shown in the app are fetched at run time from
  iNaturalist, limited to CC0 and CC BY licensed observations, and are not
  stored in this repository. Each one's observer and licence travel with it in
  the data. `lib/outside/data/taxon-reference.json` holds those references
  (URLs and attribution), not the images.
- `tests/fixtures/bsbi-reference.json`: derived from the BSBI Plant Atlas 2020,
  CC BY 4.0, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11092846>.
- `tests/fixtures/pointmoon/`: recorded responses from the Pointmoon service,
  used as test fixtures. Species observations inside them are iNaturalist
  records under the observers' CC0 or CC BY licences, attributed in the data.
- Place search uses a Nominatim-compatible geocoder when one is configured.
  OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- `lib/outside/data/phenology/`: seasonal notes written for Nature Class.
  © Johan Gace, CC BY-SA 4.0.
