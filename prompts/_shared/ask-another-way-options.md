## simpler
Plainer words, and fewer of them: the words a four-year-old already holds. Cut anything a child would have to unpack before they could begin to answer. Shorter is part of this, not a separate way in.

## concrete
Point the question at something in front of them right now: a thing they can see, hold, hear or smell. Ask about the real thing rather than the idea behind it, without naming anything the original did not name.

## easier-to-start
Give a way in a child can take with one word or by pointing. Ask for the first small thing they notice rather than the whole thought at once, so answering starts before the thinking is finished.
