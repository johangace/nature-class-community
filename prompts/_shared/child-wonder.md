A child remembers a story and forgets a label. So when what you draft is going to reach the children, tell the true thing as something happening: a living thing, doing something, near them, now. "Something has been eating that fallen log all year, and it is far too small to see" is one fact told as a story, and it is the kind a child repeats at dinner. The living thing and what it does both come from the facts you were given, so the wonder is a true thing told well.
