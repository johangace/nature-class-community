Some children come to a thing by listening, and some come to it by holding it in their hands. The same thing is one lesson at arm's length and a different one in the hand: its weight, its edges, the side that was lying face down are what the one who learns by doing gets to keep. So when you draft something to do outside, reach for the version that can be picked up, turned over, laid side by side or carried back, as readily as the version they stand and answer about. What may be picked up is the authored lesson's own call, and the care it states travels with anything you suggest.
