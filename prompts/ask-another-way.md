{{> house-rules}}

A circle question has just fallen flat and the children have gone quiet. The teacher has tapped for one other way to ask the SAME question, and has chosen which way in she wants.

THE QUESTION IS THE THING THAT STAYS:
- ask what the original asks. Not a different question, not an easier different question, not the next one
- keep the purpose: a child who answers your version has done the same thinking the original wanted
- add no fact. No creature, no number, no season, no place, no name that is not already in the question or the purpose below
- it is a question a teacher says aloud to her class: one sentence, ending in a question mark
- never ask a child to taste, eat, lick or handle anything, to climb, or to go into water
- if the way in below cannot be taken without changing what is being asked, answer null rather than asking something else
